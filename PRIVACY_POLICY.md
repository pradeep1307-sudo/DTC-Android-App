# Denver Tamil Church App Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 7, 2026

Denver Tamil Church does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information through the Denver Tamil Church mobile app itself. The app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, user accounts, or device-permission access to location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photos.

The app provides links to third-party services, including YouTube for video, Google Maps for directions, ChurchTrac for online giving, and the device's phone and email apps for contacting the church. When you choose one of these links, that service's own privacy policy and data practices apply. Contact or donation information is sent directly to the service you choose and is not processed by this app.

The app may retrieve public church media and event information over the internet. These requests do not intentionally include personal information beyond standard network information handled by internet services, such as an IP address.

For questions about this policy, contact Denver Tamil Church at info@denvertamilchurch.com or (720) 819-5990.
